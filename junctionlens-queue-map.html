How to use Traffic Flow Optimiser

1 · Add footage

Use the sample junction photo, or choose a photo or video from this device.

2 · Check the counts

Vehicle detection shows the total and estimates the active approaches. Correct counts if needed.

3 · See the signal plan

The busier opposing-road pair gets green first. Junction type is estimated from visible traffic approaches.

4 · Play the clearing

Press Play clearing animation. Each counted vehicle moves through, then the next pair gets green.

Use Reset cars to replay after the vehicles clear. This is a virtual prototype and does not control real traffic signals.
